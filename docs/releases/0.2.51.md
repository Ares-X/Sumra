# Vira Reader 0.2.51

- Opening a document from Finder at launch no longer leaves an extra Welcome window. [#169](https://github.com/Ares-X/Vira-Reader/issues/169)
- New Tab and Duplicate in New Tab show the Normal toolbar and its reading controls. [#170](https://github.com/Ares-X/Vira-Reader/issues/170)
- The fold on generic Finder and title-bar document icons now sits in front of the Vira logo. [#165](https://github.com/Ares-X/Vira-Reader/issues/165)

Apple Silicon · macOS 13 or later. The download is self-signed and distributed without Apple notarization. If Gatekeeper prompts on first launch, follow [Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445) for granting an exception to a download you trust. In-app updates use Sparkle signatures.
