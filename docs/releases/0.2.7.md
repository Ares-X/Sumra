Sumra 0.2.7 reduces reading and Contents work in large books and shows TXT search matches before the complete result count finishes.

- Give visible reading pixels priority over queued sidebar and palette previews.
- Reuse compact page geometry for equal-size comics and PDFs, preserving mixed page sizes, spreads, covers, rotation and RTL.
- Reuse measured page-size estimates and keep Contents rows across unrelated scrolling and status updates.
- Select the first TXT match during scanning; retain its exact position when counting completes, including Find Next and cancellation.
- Resolve large CHM tables of contents through one canonical topic map.
- Refresh the SumatraPDF capability and implementation comparison against the October 19 upstream tree.
- Include English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese READMEs in corresponding source.

Original books remain read-only. No whole-book persistent cache or new dependency is added. First solid-archive jumps still pay for decoding within their selected independent section, and full-book EPUB search counts can remain slow.

Requires macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon. The existing self-signed identity and Sparkle update key are retained. Corresponding source, provenance and checksums accompany the app.
