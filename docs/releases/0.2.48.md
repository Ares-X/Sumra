# Vira Reader 0.2.48

First Vira Reader release, replacing the Sumra product name. **Moving from Sumra requires installing Vira Reader.app manually once.** The new app has its own bundle identifier and update feed; the Sumra feed stays at 0.2.47.

## Changes

- Renamed the application to Vira Reader with a monochrome layered-page V icon, new repository and release filenames. First launch copies legacy preferences and reading history once, preserving existing Vira choices and leaving Sumra settings unchanged. The application and Quick Look extensions use new bundle identifiers; the existing signing certificate and Sparkle key are retained.

## Fixes

- Normal Auto Hide toolbars retain an opaque title background when the document proxy icon appears after adding or duplicating a tab. Native caption separators hide with the controls.
- Read Aloud starts correctly in inline frames and keeps word highlighting in the frame where reading began, even after focus moves elsewhere. Moving, stopping or replacing the source clears the previous word's paint; navigating or removing the source clears its highlight.
- Annotation sidebars keep their existing rows while refreshing. Editing annotations on known pages rereads those pages instead of the entire PDF; undo and form changes retain full refresh behavior.

## Known remaining issues

- Finder Quick Look arrow-key routing remains unresolved in #96. Damaged-page recovery through the preview buttons is already fixed.
- #158 remains open for confirmation on the reporter's macOS 15.7.5. Acceptance uses the existing macOS 26.6.2 VM; no macOS 15 acceptance is claimed.

Apple Silicon · macOS 13 or later. Distribution uses the project's existing self-signed, nonnotarized package and signed Sparkle updates.
