# Sumra 0.2.48

## Fixes

- Normal Auto Hide toolbars retain an opaque title background when the document proxy icon appears after adding or duplicating a tab. Native caption separators hide with the controls.
- Read Aloud starts correctly in inline frames and keeps word highlighting in the frame where reading began, even after focus moves elsewhere. Moving, stopping or replacing the source clears the previous word's paint; navigating or removing the source clears its highlight.
- Annotation sidebars keep their existing rows while refreshing. Editing annotations on known pages rereads those pages instead of the entire PDF; undo and form changes retain full refresh behavior.
