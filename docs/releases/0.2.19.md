# Sumra 0.2.19

- Correct mixed text and illustration pagination in FB2 and MOBI, preserving authored internal page breaks and avoiding empty terminal pages. Inline images and adjacent text now share the same line measurements, including vertical alignment and oversized illustrations.
- Give generated Finder thumbnails the page's proportions, while preserving authored landscape covers.
- Give revealed toolbars a background matching the reading theme. Hide the complete fullscreen toolbar surface without moving the book, including when entering fullscreen with Auto Hide or Always Hide selected.
- Keep link previews attached to the currently hovered target and dismiss them on pointer or key-window departure, including in two-page spreads.
- Keep native window controls available at the top edge in fullscreen even with reader tools set to Always Hide. Preserve interruptible toolbar fades after customization and avoid redundant reader updates while the pointer moves across an unchanged toolbar state.
- Remove the stale blue focus outline left after clicking beside a comic or cover and resizing the window. Keyboard navigation remains available.
- Keep search status updates from changing the reading viewport and restarting full-book searches. This also applies to Find Next after the find bar is closed; closing Find still returns its space to the book.
- Reduce repeated preparation during full-book search by sharing cancellation resources within each page batch and reusing the already extracted lookahead page instead of loading it again. Complete counts, precise match locations, and cancellation between pages are preserved.

On the tested Mac and reader settings, the dense 50 MB EPUB stress query returned all 6,591 matches. One quiet GUI pair measured 17.702 seconds at baseline and 16.921 seconds on the final engine from input to final state (15.231 seconds from the first `Searching… 0` status). This single 4.4% comparison is not a latency promise for other books or machines; search optimization remains under investigation.

Finder's EPUB thumbnail provider remains unresolved. Tests with 0.2.19 installed at the normal `/Applications/Sumra.app` path, with Sumra selected for all EPUB roles, and with Sumra's unique thumbnail extension elected still routed thumbnail requests to Apple Books. The covered control produced a thumbnail; the coverless EPUB retained a generic icon. Sumra's Quick Look preview displays the coverless EPUB's first page. Provider selection remains under investigation.
