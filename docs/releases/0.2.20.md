# Sumra 0.2.20

- Keep ebook pagination, search locations and highlights consistent after rendering other pages or changing render scale.
- Speed up full-book search in chapter-based ebooks by sharing chapters dynamically between two document contexts. Complete counts, cross-chapter highlights and result navigation are preserved; canceled or outdated searches cannot replace current results.
- Repeating an unchanged ebook search that already completed with no matches responds immediately, without scanning the book again. Changing search options or layout still starts a new search; selection-only misses cannot suppress later whole-book results.
- Enlarge and center standalone FB2/MOBI title pages as the reading canvas grows. Fit eligible title pages to their content instead of retaining ordinary text-page margins, keeping the title, author and artwork together while respecting explicit image sizes and publisher spacing.
- Keep the page point under a pinch gesture anchored during zoom, preventing the page from sliding away as its scale changes.
- Make the native tab bar follow toolbar Auto Hide and Always Hide behavior. Revealed tabs use an opaque theme background without repaginating or shifting the book. Native overflow controls also disappear in narrow windows. Fullscreen system-menu reveal restores window controls while Always Hide and presentation keep reader tools and tabs hidden.
- Reserve stable space for the native toolbar and tabs in every reading mode. Fullscreen also reserves the system menu's height, so revealing the controls does not cover the book title or move the page.
- Add a compact bottom reading-progress control. Hover or focus reveals the percentage, current position and draggable slider without changing the reading area. Preview percentages match the destination page; a drag commits one jump only when its destination changes. Partially paginated books seek directly into the target chapter without laying out the whole book. Text shows source lines and browser content shows progress within the current file.
- Recover automatically when a generated PDF is replaced during opening, as can happen with Typst or LaTeX builds. Keep the displayed PDF and reading position while reopening the latest source after the existing file-change delay. Other open errors remain visible, and a canceled open or new unsaved edits prevent a stale retry from replacing the reader.
- Apply theme changes across all reader windows. Reopening a book keeps the current global theme instead of restoring a theme saved with its reading position.
- Reuse an empty welcome window when opening a file; otherwise open another reader, using native tabs when macOS settings request them. Existing books stay open.

In local release-configuration GUI checks on the same 50 MB EPUB, counting all 6,591 matches took 9.44 seconds, compared with 16.92 seconds in 0.2.19. Resizing during the count, including a full restart for the new layout, completed in 13.33 seconds. These measurements describe the tested Mac and document, not a latency promise for other books or machines.

The title-page, global-theme and file-opening changes address [earlier feedback in #42](https://github.com/Ares-X/Sumra/issues/42#issuecomment-6415229740); the tab-bar change addresses the [latest report](https://github.com/Ares-X/Sumra/issues/42#issuecomment-6416106998). The generated-PDF change addresses [#48](https://github.com/Ares-X/Sumra/issues/48). Physical trackpad acceptance has not been completed. The coverless EPUB Finder-thumbnail suggestion remains unresolved on the tested macOS version, which selects Apple Books for EPUB thumbnails. Sumra's Quick Look preview continues to display the first page.

Thanks to @HomeStarRunnerTron for the illustrated samples and continued interaction feedback, and @2924661311 for reporting the generated-PDF reload race and proposing a fix.
