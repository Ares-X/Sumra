# Sumra 0.2.39

- Restore the system green-button menu in floating reading controls while keeping their background shadow. Refresh traffic-light hover symbols after window and tracking-area changes.
- Resample the pointer when switching fullscreen toolbar visibility modes, and follow the native controls when AppKit moves them into the fullscreen companion window.
- Set the first loading Home window's native tab policy before attaching additional documents, avoiding a stale Home policy during fullscreen batch opening.
- Keep the browsed folder, search and selection after opening it as image pages fails. Disable hidden browser keyboard input while loading.
- Recognize an outward page-turn gesture during edge rebound without treating the rebound itself as available panning space.

The remaining reports stay open: macOS 15.7.5 behavior and physical trackpad rebound are not yet verified, brief fullscreen transition clipping needs further observation, and Finder Quick Look arrow-key routing remains unresolved (#137, #96). Native tab widths remaining fixed until pointer exit were also reproduced in a standalone AppKit application.
