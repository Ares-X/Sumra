Sumra 0.2.8 reduces solid-comic extraction overhead and large EPUB search counting costs.

- Solid entries decode directly into their final allocation and use hardware CRC32 on Apple Silicon. Independent LZMA2 section seeking, full CRC checks and original files are preserved.
- Finite EPUB searches inspect page-local flow groups before extracting glyphs. Long paragraphs no longer require a whole-paragraph scan for every page.
- Continuous EPUB searches use painted text order to reject impossible CJK phrases before extracting whole-chapter glyphs. Complete counting skips empty pages inside the existing document actor while preserving cancellation and exact results.
- Repeated EPUB raster resources reuse MuPDF's bounded in-memory store instead of being inflated for every chapter. Full path/archive identity, resource lifetime and SVG behavior are preserved.
- Chapter completion that leaves page numbers unchanged no longer repeatedly restores the viewport or republishes search results during counting.

No original book is changed and no whole-book persistent cache is added. First seeks inside a solid LZMA2 section still require sequential decoding; this release reduces overhead rather than promising instant arbitrary jumps. Large EPUBs can still spend time parsing and laying out candidate chapters. See the [detailed measurements and limits](https://github.com/Ares-X/Sumra/blob/main/docs/SEEK_SEARCH_PERFORMANCE_20261020.md).
