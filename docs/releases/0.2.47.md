# Sumra 0.2.47

## Fixes

- Normal Auto Hide toolbars keep an opaque document-themed background after adding or duplicating tabs.
- Fullscreen reader chrome releases the replaced system tab row and keeps the custom row at its intended size. Toolbars and tabs paint their own bounds without a separate companion backing, shadow or separator.
- Cancelling HTML/CHM full-document text preparation stops its WebKit reads. A delayed old result cannot replace a new request.
- Text export and Ask Document include accessible inline/nested srcdoc text, including unvisited chapters, without duplicating indexed topics or reading opaque sandbox frames.
- TXT thumbnails stop reading after a bounded preview prefix, including documents containing exceptionally long Unicode combining sequences.
