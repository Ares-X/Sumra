# Sumra 0.2.38

- Keep diagonal scrolling inside a zoomed page instead of unexpectedly turning pages midway through the gesture.
- Make floating-tab hover and close-button feedback consistent across the whole tab.
- Hide the native fullscreen toolbar's background together with its content when switching to automatic hiding.
- Clarify folder browsing and reading images as pages. Browsed folders appear in Recents immediately and reopen in the appropriate mode. Empty image folders list the supported image types.
- Distinguish bookmarks at different positions on the same PDF page or CHM topic, including older PDF bookmark coordinates.
- Preserve the default CHM homepage anchor on first open while retaining saved reading positions.
- Let a clicked Quick Look page receive keyboard navigation without taking focus when the preview first opens.

The remaining macOS 15 fullscreen hover, transition-clipping and tab/Space reports in #137 are still being investigated. This release does not establish that all of #137 is resolved.
