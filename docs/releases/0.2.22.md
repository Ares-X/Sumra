# Sumra 0.2.22

- Add optional floating reading controls with compact navigation, document tabs and reading tools. Narrow windows combine them into one row; long names keep their beginning and extension. Single-book titles use the available width and multiple document tabs share it before scrolling.
- Keep Home separate from document tabs, with a fixed inset header. New Tab selects books before opening them, Cancel leaves the current reader unchanged, and Home preserves open books. Restore a reader or Home when reopening the running app from the Dock after windows close.
- Keep Auto Hide controls available when switching tabs, opening a book beneath a stationary pointer, or entering the View menu. Handle windowless mouse-moved screen coordinates after full-screen transitions. Preserve native window buttons and stable page geometry in ordinary and full-screen windows.
- Prevent the native full-screen toolbar from covering the floating controls during system-menu use and document switches. Keep window buttons clear of the macOS window-sharing indicator.
- Retain top-edge window controls in Always Hide mode, and keep tab switching, batch closing and saved groups complete when the system tab bar is hidden.
- Reflow remaining tabs immediately after closing a middle tab. Make New Tab available from the toolbar, Tabs menu and Command-T, and provide New Window, New Tab and Replace Current opening preferences.
- Add Narrow, Medium and Wide page-margin presets. Reader-generated title-page margins stay compact as the window grows, while authored spacing, image proportions, chapter pagination and search locations remain intact.
- Keep facing pages synchronized during live window resizing, preserve the reading passage after final reflow, and anchor zoom to the gesture location or viewport center.
- Apply reading settings immediately to open books and use the latest defaults when opening another book. Field-level migration preserves valid per-book choices without restoring settings that the user has since changed globally. Settings categories now include system icons.
- Add configurable page-divider color and visibility, with a matching live two-page theme preview. Global themes update reading windows, Home and Settings together.
- Provide Always Show, Auto Hide and Always Hide progress controls, with a wider track and balanced fixed padding. The bottom hover area is reachable without touching the Dock, and the last facing-page spread reaches 100%.
- Keep the selected progress preview until its navigation finishes. Rapid drags and continuous reading no longer flash back to an older position when an earlier seek completes.
- Preserve dragged sidebar widths when saving and reloading a PDF, including right-side and AI layouts. Explicit sidebar-width settings still update the open reader immediately.
- Keep the document pane readable beside a sidebar in narrow windows, and restore the preferred sidebar width when widening the window again.
- Navigate the complete topic sequence when dragging CHM progress. Display long page and chapter counters in full, and keep the track still when a chapter finishes paginating during a drag.
- Preserve selected search results and their count together when lazy pagination changes page numbers. Show loading feedback while a requested comic page is decoding.
- Reserve the selected floating-control clearance for fixed pages as well as reflowable books.
- Keep adjacent Markdown headings with their first body text, preserve nested Contents entries, and migrate existing reading positions and bookmarks to the revised chapter layout.
- Avoid repeatedly scanning text prefixes when checking whole-word boundaries and mapping normalized search results back to Unicode source positions. Preserve complete result counts and precise forward/backward navigation.

Existing reading preferences migrate automatically. Floating controls remain optional and off by default; the standard layout retains modest reading margins.

Distribution continues to use the existing community code signature and signed Sparkle updates. The release is not Apple-notarized. A coverless EPUB may retain Finder's generic icon when macOS selects another thumbnail provider; Quick Look remains available.
